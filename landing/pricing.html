FAQ SUITE

Frequently Asked Questions

Here is the essential information on XPIDER software purchases and licensing terms most frequently asked by buyers.

Can I run the scraper on multiple devices with a single license?

A single subscription key is bound to a single PC profile session. For agencies requiring concurrent executions, we highly recommend the Enterprise Plan to scale independent multi-browser seats and ports.

Does a special filter operate to lower spam blocks when sending bulk emails?

Yes, indeed. SendForce Mailer Pro features an integrated sending delay scheduler and SMTP proxy-switching system to eliminate spam blocks and domain overload, keeping email success rates at their peak.

Does the XPIDER VPN proxy stay active for general web browsing other than scraping?

XPIDER VPN employs a built-in proxy relay, encrypting all outbound traffic and web browsing inside XPIDER. The target web servers will always see secure proxy IPs instead of your real residential IP.

Can I switch my plan from Starter to Business Pro in the middle of a subscription?

Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time with a single click. The upgrade will be prorated, meaning you only pay the exact difference for the remaining period, and the high-end modules unlock instantly.

What are the refund guarantee conditions and procedures?

If you experience scraping issues or software difficulties within 14 days of purchase, we offer a 100% guarantee refund under our usage limits. Simply contact support with your email and receipt number, and it will be processed within the day.
