FAQ SUITE

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about XPIDER B2B license acquisition, usage rules, and configuration guidelines.

Can I run scrapers on multiple devices with a single license?

A single standard license key is bound to one system profile instance. For large agencies running concurrent workflows across multiple seats, we highly recommend acquiring the Enterprise Plan which unlocks unlimited concurrent profiles and port expansions.

Does the mailer bypass spam detection servers?

Yes. SendForce Mailer Pro features intelligent outbox delay timers and rotating SMTP headers to proactively prevent domain blacklist triggers and maximize inbox deliverability metrics.

Is the proxy active outside the crawler?

XPIDER VPN encapsulates the integrated browser pipeline, meaning all tab queries and outbound extraction packets within XPIDER are completely encrypted. External sites only perceive the proxy IP node.

Can I upgrade from Starter to Business Pro mid-subscription?

Yes, you can upgrade instantly through your dashboard. The billing portal will dynamically calculate the pro-rated cost difference, immediately unlocking premium capabilities.

How do I process a refund guarantee?

If you encounter technical system defects within 14 days of purchase, submit a ticket with your order ID. Our billing department will verify the logs and execute a 100% refund on the same business day.
